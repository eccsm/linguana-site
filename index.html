Linguana

Learn a new language by having real conversations. Linguana is a mobile app for Android and iOS where you practice everyday situations with an AI conversation partner.

What is Linguana?

Linguana helps you build speaking confidence in a foreign language. Instead of memorizing word lists, you step into realistic roleplay scenarios, like ordering at a cafe, checking into a hotel, or visiting a doctor, and hold a conversation with an AI character in the language you are learning.

As you chat, Linguana can correct your grammar and suggest more natural phrasing, so you learn from every message you send.

Features

Real-world roleplay

Practice scenarios such as cafes, restaurants, shopping, airports, hotels, asking for directions, doctor visits, phone calls, and job interviews.

Tutor and Roleplay modes

Get instant corrections and feedback in Tutor mode, or stay in character without interruptions in Roleplay mode.

Voice practice

Speak out loud and hear natural-sounding replies to train your listening and pronunciation.

Games and progress

Keep a daily streak, play word puzzles, earn rewards, and climb weekly leagues to stay motivated.

Signing in with Google

Linguana offers "Sign in with Google" so you can create an account quickly. When you sign in with Google, Linguana only requests your basic profile information: your name, email address, and profile picture. We use this information solely to create and identify your Linguana account and to save your learning progress across devices.

Linguana does not sell your Google user data, does not use it for advertising, and does not share it with third parties except the services needed to run the app (such as Firebase Authentication). For full details, read our Privacy Policy.

Pricing
