Account

Delete your Linguana account

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Linguana and sign in.
  2. Tap Me in the bottom bar.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm twice. Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

Can't open the app?

Email ekincan@casim.net from the email address on your Linguana account with the subject "Delete my Linguana account". We'll confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, username, email address and sign-in
  • Your conversations and chat history
  • Vocabulary cards and review history
  • XP, streaks, gems, items, avatars and in-app purchase records stored with your account
  • Daily and weekly challenge scores and league entries
  • Feedback you sent from the app

What is kept

  • Prize records: If you won a daily or weekly prize, the record of that win (user ID, display name and score) is kept to prevent fraud
  • Crash reports: Anonymous crash logs expire after up to 90 days
  • Purchases: Google Play, the App Store and RevenueCat keep transaction records as required for billing and tax

Subscriptions: deleting your account doesn't cancel Linguana Pro. Cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) or in your Apple ID settings.
