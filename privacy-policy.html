Introduction

Linguana ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the Linguana mobile application, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal information.

Information We Collect

1. Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Name or display name, and the username you choose
  • A user ID assigned by Firebase Authentication
  • Your sign-in method: email and password, Google, Apple or Facebook. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us. With Google, Apple or Facebook sign-in we receive your name and email address from that provider.

2. Conversations

  • What: The messages you write or speak in chats, and the replies Linguana's AI characters send you
  • Why: To generate replies, show corrections when Tutor mode is on, keep your chat history, and build your vocabulary review cards
  • Processing: To generate a reply, your recent conversation is sent to our AI provider (OpenAI). When you hear a reply read aloud, the reply text is sent to our voice provider (ElevenLabs) to create the audio
  • Storage: Conversations are saved to your account so you can continue them later, until you delete your account
  • Important: Please don't share sensitive personal information in chats. Anything you write is processed as described here

3. Voice Input (Microphone Permission)

Important: Linguana never records, uploads or stores your voice.

  • How it works: When you tap the microphone, your speech is converted to text by your device's built-in speech recognition (provided by Google on Android and by Apple on iOS). Linguana receives only the resulting text, which is then handled like a typed message
  • When: Only while you hold or tap the microphone button
  • Control: You can revoke microphone permission at any time in your device settings and keep typing instead

4. Learning Progress and Activity

To run lessons, games and leagues, we store:

  • Your languages, level and preferences (such as theme, voice and Tutor mode)
  • XP, streaks, gems, items, avatars and purchases you make in the app
  • Scores in daily and weekly challenges, league stars and leaderboard positions
  • Vocabulary cards and review history
  • Feedback you send us through the app

5. Device and Diagnostic Information

  • Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics collects crash logs and basic device details (model, OS version, app version) so we can fix bugs
  • Push notifications: If you allow notifications, we store a Firebase Cloud Messaging token to send reminders. You can turn notifications off in your device settings

6. Purchases

  • Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Google Play or the App Store. We never receive your card or payment details
  • RevenueCat receives your purchase history and app user ID to confirm what you've bought

7. Advertising ID

  • What it is: A resettable identifier provided by Google Play services or iOS
  • How it's used: Google AdMob uses it to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. Linguana requests non-personalized ads only
  • Location: We do not collect your precise location. Advertising partners may infer an approximate location (such as your country or city) from your IP address
  • Control: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings

8. Conversation Context for Sponsored Placements

  • Purpose: To decide whether a contextually relevant sponsored placement is available after a chat response
  • Data transmitted: Your most recent message, Linguana's reply, an app key, a randomly generated conversation-session identifier, and standard network information such as your IP address
  • How it's used: Zeekend matches the current conversation context to eligible sponsored content and measures requests, impressions, and clicks
  • Important: Conversation text is sent as written. If you include personal information in a message, that information may be included in the text sent for matching
  • Not transmitted by this integration: Your Linguana account ID, email address, phone number, advertising ID, device ID, cookies, system prompt, or earlier conversation turns
  • Your control: Tap the Sponsored label on a placement to see why it was shown. Once it has been displayed you can hide it with ✕ or from that panel

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide conversations, lessons, games, leagues and your account across devices
  • Personalize practice to your languages and level
  • Prevent cheating, fraud and abuse, and keep leaderboards fair
  • Analyze app usage and performance, and fix bugs and crashes
  • Show ads that keep the free version free
  • Send reminders and important app notifications, if you allow them
  • Respond to your feedback and support requests

What Other Users Can See

Your display name or username, avatar and scores appear on leaderboards and in your weekly league, where other Linguana users can see them. Your email address, conversations and other account details are never shown to other users.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information:

  • All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and stored encrypted at rest by Google Cloud
  • Database access rules limit each account to its own data, and our servers verify who you are before acting on your account
  • Secure authentication via Firebase
  • No recording or storage of your voice

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you, and get a copy of it
  • Correct your name, username and preferences in the app
  • Delete your account and associated data (see below)
  • Revoke permissions (microphone, notifications) at any time via device settings
  • Object to or restrict certain processing

To make a request, email ekincan@casim.net from the address on your account.

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open Me → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, conversations, vocabulary, progress, gems, items, scores and league entries, and removes your sign-in. If you can't access the app, see how to request deletion.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Linguana Pro subscription: manage or cancel it in Google Play or the App Store.

Children's Privacy

Linguana is intended for users aged 13 and over, and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

Data Retention

  • Voice: Never recorded or stored
  • Account, conversations and progress: Kept until you delete your account, then deleted from our systems
  • Prize records: Records of past daily and weekly prize winners (user ID, display name and score) are kept to prevent fraud
  • Crash reports: Kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days
  • Service providers: Providers such as OpenAI may keep request data for a limited time under their own policies, for example to prevent abuse

Additional Information for Specific Permissions

RECORD_AUDIO Permission

  • Why we need it: To turn your speech into text for speaking practice
  • When we use it: Only while you use the microphone button
  • What we do: Your device's speech recognition converts speech to text; the app receives only the text
  • What we DON'T do: Record, upload, store or share your voice

INTERNET Permission

  • Why we need it: To sign you in, sync progress, generate replies and load content
  • Data transmitted: Encrypted authentication tokens, conversations, learning progress and diagnostics, as described above

ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Permission

  • Why we need it: To check network connectivity before syncing data

READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Permission

  • Why we need it: To access downloaded learning materials and content
  • What we access: Only app-specific files and user-selected content. Nothing is uploaded

AD_ID Permission

  • Why we need it: Required for apps using Google AdMob advertising on Android 13+
  • What it does: Lets AdMob read the Advertising ID for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention. Linguana requests non-personalized ads only
  • Your control: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in device settings
